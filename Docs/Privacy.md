**Privacy Policy**

This privacy policy applies to the ScheduleSage app (the "Application") on Apple platforms including macOS and iOS, created by Hangzhou Quest Technology Co., Ltd. (the "Service Provider"). This policy is effective immediately and describes how we collect, use, and share your information when you use the Application "as is".

**Information Collection and Use**

The Application collects certain information when you download and use it, including but not limited to:

- Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.
- Usage data, such as the pages you visit in the Application, the date and time of access, and the duration of use.
- System information, such as the operating system version installed on your device.

The Application also collects your device's approximate location (not precise GPS coordinates) to provide:

- **Geolocation Services:** To tailor personalized content, offer relevant recommendations, and enable location-based features.
- **Analytics and Improvements:** To analyze user behavior using aggregated and anonymized data and enhance the overall performance and functionality of the Application.
- **Third-Party Services:** To occasionally transmit anonymized location data to external services for analytical and optimization purposes.

For an enhanced user experience, the Application may require you to provide certain personally identifiable information (e.g., your Email address). The information you provide is securely stored and retained only as long as necessary and will be used consistent with this privacy policy.

The Application does not collect any advertising identifiers or track your activity across other apps and websites.

**Third-Party Access and Data Sharing**

We may share aggregated or anonymized data with trusted third-party service providers to improve the Application's performance. Detailed information regarding the third-party services we use is provided below:

- **Sentry:**  
  The Application uses Sentry to collect crash logs and diagnostic data in order to analyze and improve the performance and stability of the App. The data collected (such as error stack traces, device information, and usage details) is processed in an anonymized and aggregated manner to protect your privacy. For more details about data handling and available control measures, please refer to Sentry's [Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/).

- **RevenueCat:**  
  The Application uses RevenueCat to manage and process in-app purchase transactions. RevenueCat collects transaction data, receipts, and subscription status to verify purchases and enable premium content functionalities. More information regarding their data handling practices is available in RevenueCat's [Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/). You may manage your purchase and subscription details through your App Store account or contact support for further assistance.

- **Tencent Hunyuan Large Model:**  
  The Application integrates an advanced AI service powered by Tencent's Hunyuan large model to perform schedule recognition and analysis based on user input. When using the AI features, your schedule data (including event details, timings, and related metadata) may be transmitted securely to Tencent's servers for processing using industry-standard encryption protocols. The processed data is used solely to enhance schedule recognition accuracy and provide personalized recommendations. We ensure that the data is anonymized as much as possible during processing. For more details on how Tencent handles data, please review [Tencent Privacy Policy](https://privacy.tencent.com/).

User-provided and automatically collected information may also be disclosed:

- When required by law, such as in response to a subpoena or similar legal process.
- When we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.
- With trusted service providers who work on our behalf, under strict confidentiality obligations.
